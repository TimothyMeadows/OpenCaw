# WEB_SKEUOMORPHIC.md

## Intent

Use restrained physical cues to make unfamiliar digital controls understandable without sacrificing modern accessibility or responsiveness.

## Production Rules

- Apply material cues only when they explain affordance, state, or manipulation.
- Define a small material system covering surface, edge, recess, elevation, light direction, and interaction state.
- Keep labels and icons semantic; do not require users to recognize an obsolete physical object.
- Replace expensive texture layers with scalable gradients, borders, and reusable assets where practical.
- Provide flat and reduced-motion fallbacks that preserve all state communication.

## Acceptance Checks

- Controls remain understandable with texture, shadow, and animation removed.
- Pressed, selected, disabled, focused, and error states cannot be confused.
- Decorative depth does not reduce contrast, target size, or responsive adaptability.
- Asset and rendering costs remain within the host budget.

## Role Fit

Use with `web-experience-designer`, `frontend-developer`, `art-director`, and `qa-engineer`.
