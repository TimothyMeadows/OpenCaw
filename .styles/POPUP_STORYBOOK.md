# POPUP_STORYBOOK.md

## Intent

Design responsive interfaces and scenes as a bound paper object with readable leaves, gutters, foldouts, and pop-up stages.

## Production Rules

- Define cover, binding, leaf, page edge, gutter, fold line, tab, foldout, and pop-up construction consistently.
- Use a two-page spread when space permits and a deliberate single-page or stacked-leaf composition on narrow viewports.
- Keep critical text, controls, faces, and interaction targets outside gutters, folds, trim, hinges, and likely hand-occlusion areas.
- Establish reading and focus order independently from visual page turns so keyboard and assistive navigation remain predictable.
- Make page turns and pop-up motion restrained, interruptible, and nonessential; provide equivalent reduced-motion state changes.
- Preserve touch target size, contrast, runtime text, responsive safe areas, and state visibility over decorative realism.

## Acceptance Checks

- Covers, bindings, leaves, gutters, folds, shadows, and pop-up mechanics form one plausible paper system.
- Single-page and two-page layouts preserve content order, focus, target size, and meaning.
- Nothing essential is clipped, buried in the gutter, hidden behind a foldout, or dependent on motion alone.
- Geometry, aspect ratios, open and closed states, focus states, and reduced-motion equivalents are complete.

## Role Fit

Use with `papercraft-art-director`, `web-experience-designer`, `art-director`, and accessibility-aware implementation roles.
