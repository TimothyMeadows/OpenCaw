# WEB_LIGHT_PAPER.md

## Intent

Create calm, content-forward web interfaces with the clarity of printed matter, generous light surfaces, and restrained tactile depth.

## Production Rules

- Use warm near-white surfaces, dark neutral text, and one deliberate accent family.
- Build hierarchy through type scale, spacing, rules, columns, and subtle tonal panels before adding shadows.
- Keep borders fine but visible, corners modest, and imagery editorial rather than decorative.
- Reserve elevated cards for temporary or interactive layers; keep primary reading surfaces flat.
- Provide dark-environment and high-contrast alternatives when the host product requires them.

## Acceptance Checks

- Long-form text remains comfortable at narrow and wide measures.
- Interactive controls are distinguishable without relying on hover or shadow alone.
- Surface boundaries remain visible under low contrast, zoom, and grayscale review.
- The page preserves hierarchy with imagery disabled and motion reduced.

## Role Fit

Use with `web-experience-designer`, `frontend-developer`, `technical-writer`, and `art-director`.
