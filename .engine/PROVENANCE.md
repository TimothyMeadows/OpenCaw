The constant-output design analyzer was not imported or recreated. Structural report checks validate evidence shape and preserve failures/unavailable cases; they cannot score fun or establish gameplay correctness. API fixtures and simulated processes do not certify native engine execution or physically felt vibration.

Engine/API references are primary vendor documentation linked from definitions and feature skills. Host lockfiles, Editor versions, renderers, input/UI stacks and platform support govern actual implementation. Optional paid services/plugins remain separate selections. No AI model, external application, hosted service or paid middleware is added by this catalog.
