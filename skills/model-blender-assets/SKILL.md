# Model Blender Assets

## When to use

Use for Blender blockout, hard-surface, organic, sculpting, retopology, topology-cleanup, silhouette, or deformation-mesh work.

## Workflow

1. Freeze target scale, camera distance, silhouette, deformation needs, and measured geometry budget.
2. Block out primary masses before detail; compare against approved references without tracing protected expression.
3. Choose hard-surface, organic, sculpt-and-retopo, or modular construction deliberately.
4. Maintain intentional topology, normals, manifold boundaries, naming, transforms, pivots, and modifier ownership.
5. Inspect silhouette, shading, intersections, thickness, deformation loops, and subdivision behavior at checkpoints.
6. Hand the approved working copy to UV, rigging, procedural, or export stages without overwriting its source.

Read [modeling-and-topology-contract.md](references/modeling-and-topology-contract.md) for topology and evidence requirements.

## Guardrails

- Do not add invisible density or destructive detail before proportions are approved.
- Do not treat modifier previews as applied delivery state.
- Do not approve non-manifold, self-intersecting, or deformation-hostile topology without a recorded exception.
