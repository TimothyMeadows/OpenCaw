# Subject construction and hidden surfaces

## Resolve the subject before topology

Record intended use, supported camera range, scale, required poses and permitted stylization. Separate observable landmarks from supported structural inference and unresolved areas. For PROOF_TO_MODEL, classification belongs to registered original sources; generated views and candidate renders cannot become evidence about the original. Keep its proof approval and fixed finishing contract authoritative.

Use a landmark sheet to compare height, width, depth, major axis directions, support/contact points and attachment positions. A concealed limb or rear panel requires an explicit construction decision. Do not silently omit it, copy visible geometry into an implausible location or claim an inferred hidden form is an exact reconstruction. Resolve uncertainty that changes identity, articulation or fit before fine detail.

## Select the appropriate construction checks

| Subject | Decisions and working checks | Handoff evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deforming character | Torso/limb masses, joint centers, head and facial planes, equipment attachment, expression and silhouette. Place edge flow around actual bending and closing regions; avoid high-valence poles in folds. | Bind/neutral pose and specified shoulder, elbow, hip, knee, hand and facial extremes; intended clothing/equipment clearance. |
| Creature | Species-appropriate segment order, limb attachment, stance, mouth/eye placement, tail/wing articulation and support. Use sourced anatomy for the subject; do not impose human proportions or a universal leg arrangement. | Approved landmark comparison, grounded stance, declared limb/wing ranges and concealed-surface review. |
| Vehicle or machine | Chassis/body dimensions, wheelbase or other support layout, access panels, propulsion/steering assemblies, interiors visible through openings, mechanical stops and service gaps. | Part/parent/pivot ledger, wheel/steering/suspension or other selected movement ranges, underside/cavity views and target scale. |

A mechanical animal belongs to the nonliving assembly path unless the brief explicitly requests deforming surfaces. Decorative anatomical shapes do not require an invented humanoid skeleton. Conversely, an organic character with a plausible silhouette is not ready to skin until its deformation zones are inspected.

## Build and verify

1. Block out masses and joints with enough separation to test proportions and contacts. Review silhouette at the actual gameplay/render distance before sculpting pores, engravings or small feathers.
2. Build the surfaces needed for approved poses and camera views. Maintain thickness at openings, independent movable pieces, attachment support and intentional cavities. Remove hidden geometry only under a documented visibility/motion policy.
3. Test symmetry as a construction aid, then retain intentional asymmetry from the approved design. Mirrored UVs, equipment and topology need explicit ownership rather than accidental duplicate surfaces.
4. Use temporary pose or articulation checks on declared working copies to reveal pinching, penetration and newly exposed holes. Record missing surfaces as modeling repairs; do not pass them to rigging as animation problems.
5. Deliver landmark/part records, topology views, measured geometry totals and unresolved findings with the model. Rigging owns final control/deform construction and motion evidence; export owns actual consumer verification.

Accept against the requested subject, style and use. Anatomy ratios, fixed polygon allocations and a clean wireframe cannot replace source comparison or actual pose checks. A single front render cannot certify the back, underside, mouth interior or occluded attachments.
