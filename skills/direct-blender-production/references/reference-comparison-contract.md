# Reference comparison and detail checkpoints

## Establish a fair comparison

Use approved, rights-cleared originals as references. Record which properties are requirements under STYLE.md: proportions, silhouette, material family, construction or detail language. Record visible evidence, supported hidden-surface inference and unresolved assumptions separately. A generated view or model render cannot establish a fact about an original in PROOF_TO_MODEL.

Match projection, viewing direction, framing and subject scale before diagnosing geometry. Record uncertain focal length or perspective as an assumption; do not distort a correct model to fit one uncertain camera. Keep a reproducible neutral-light setup, exposure and color transform alongside the intended final presentation. Compare the same settings and artifact identities between revisions.

## Work from structure to detail

1. Compare primary masses and negative spaces in silhouette at the intended viewing distance. Correct proportion and attachment problems before decoration.
2. Review secondary construction: panel overlaps, seams, joints, wall thickness and plausible concealed surfaces. Check each moving assembly through its intended clearance range.
3. Inspect declared detail priorities in close-ups and at delivery size. Gear teeth must have readable, consistent spacing and clearance; feather or plate layers need intentional overlap, thickness and clean terminations. Repetition should express construction, without accidental intersections, melted joins or ornamental noise hiding a damaged base.
4. Compare neutral-light material response: roughness, metal/dielectric separation, bevel highlights, texture frequency and emissive accents. Keep a beauty view, wireframe and neutral diagnostic distinct; one cannot prove the others.
5. Inspect the independently re-imported export in the selected target. A Blender-only fix may disappear through unsupported modifiers, nodes, missing textures or animation conversion.

Request an applicable subject reference from the modeling, procedural, rig or simulation owner instead of imposing one universal anatomical or mechanical checklist. Human selection of a proof, native hierarchy, motion and action remain distinct approvals in their respective workflows.

## Review record

Bind the reviewed source/export hashes, view/camera settings, target size and reviewer to the record. For each discrepancy name the part, expected and observed property, severity, image/frame evidence, likely stage owner, proposed correction and retest. Keep unresolved inference visible. Report blocked/not-run checks explicitly. A fixed iteration count, attractive screenshot or passing scene schema cannot establish visual acceptance.
