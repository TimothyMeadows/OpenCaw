# Light and Frame Blender Scenes

## When to use

Use when a Blender scene needs cameras, lenses, composition, exposure, lighting rigs, final-view readability, or render framing.

## Workflow

1. Read the active style and output contract; identify subject, hierarchy, mood, camera distance, aspect ratio, and delivery views.
2. Choose physical or intentionally stylized camera, lens, sensor, clipping, depth-of-field, and motion policy.
3. Establish exposure and color management before tuning light intensity or material response.
4. Build named, reusable light rigs with bounded samples, shadows, volumes, and reflections.
5. Review silhouette, focal hierarchy, navigation or action readability, contrast, safe areas, and responsive crops.
6. Record camera and lighting settings with representative stills before render handoff.

Read [camera-lighting-contract.md](references/camera-lighting-contract.md) for shot and evidence requirements.

## Guardrails

- Do not compensate for incorrect materials with unstable exposure or arbitrary light energy.
- Do not approve only one viewport framing when multiple output ratios are required.
- Do not sacrifice subject or gameplay readability for atmosphere.
