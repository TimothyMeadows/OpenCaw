# Reviewed rigid-part preparation

Read the canonical [rigid-parts contract](../../../runtime/bubo/resources/proof-to-model/RIGID_PARTS.md).
Select this method explicitly through schemaVersion2 finishing settings. It is
appropriate to rigid/articulated geometry, not an automatic replacement for
deforming-skin, cloth or subdivision topology.

Preserve raw reconstruction and the detailed editable master. Author real part
ownership, concealed surfaces and pivots through the approved connected Blender
workflow. Record deliberate repairs separately from LOW reduction. Keep all
authored HIGH surfaces; an already reduced proxy cannot be renamed HIGH to
manufacture a passing error measurement.

Bind every HIGH/LOW pair, parent, pivot, geometry/error budget, material policy
and collision applicability in one prepared handoff. Preserve exact unchanged
parts or bake reduced parts through real shared atlas groups. Inspect original
sources and actual surfaces; a raw-file hash alone cannot prove repair ancestry.

Obtain independent technical review of exact handoff and authoring evidence
before native execution. It is separate from human model/hierarchy/motion and
runtime acceptance. Missing memory enforcement, dependencies, applicable
settings or current input review must block. Preserve failed attempts and
explicit recovery budgets; do not restart, switch methods or expand tolerances
automatically.

Optional `rigidParts.lods.contactPolicy: protected-contact-rings-v1` requires
the existing reviewed source-corner topology receipt and its exact authoring-source
binding. Omission preserves the current whole-contact-boundary retention path.
Keep all negative cavities exact and protect complete contact one-ring triangle
positions, winding, material/UV data and distinct original owner IDs. Simplify
surrounding positive geometry only through the existing finite guards plus exact
within-boundary improper-intersection rejection. Never coordinate-weld owners or
infer cross-boundary clearance/visual quality. Require actual reduction,
independent reimport and full UV/LOD/package preflight; retain all selected
triangle/material/4K/GLB budgets and normal/fit/action tolerances unchanged.

Inspect actual exported transforms, hierarchy, UV/material/texture mappings,
LODs, colliders and independently re-imported geometry. Curate a reviewed LOD0
animation handoff; do not render alternate LODs/colliders together. Schema or
fixture success does not establish source likeness or production quality.
