# Design Conversion Pages

## When to use

- Creating or revising a landing or pricing page.
- Improving a product journey with unclear value or proof.
- Designing conversion structure, copy hierarchy, comparison, or FAQ behavior.

## Workflow

1. Define the audience, traffic context, offer, primary action, eligibility, proof, risks, and real constraints.
2. Match the headline and first action to user intent and source context.
3. Build the argument from problem and outcome through evidence, operation, objections, terms, and next action.
4. Make plan differences, costs, renewal behavior, limitations, and cancellation implications understandable.
5. Use honest specificity and verifiable proof; remove unsupported claims and artificial urgency.
6. Test comprehension, keyboard flow, responsive comparison, analytics boundaries, and post-action expectations.

## Output

- A decision-ready page outline and content hierarchy.
- Component, proof, comparison, FAQ, and state requirements.
- Ethical conversion and accessibility acceptance criteria.

## Guardrails

- Do not use preselected consent, hidden fees, false scarcity, disguised ads, or obstructed cancellation.
- Do not invent customer proof, performance numbers, or guarantees.
- Do not optimize conversion at the expense of informed consent.
